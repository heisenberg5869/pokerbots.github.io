# Glossary

**Abstraction.** Merging similar situations (hands or bet sizes) so a game becomes small enough to solve.

**Behavior strategy.** A probability distribution over actions at each information set.

**Best response.** A strategy that maximizes payoff against a fixed opponent strategy.

**Chance node.** A point where nature acts, e.g. dealing cards.

**CFR (counterfactual regret minimization).** Iterative self-play algorithm whose average strategy converges to equilibrium in two-player zero-sum games.

**Exploitability.** How much a best-responding opponent gains over the game value against your strategy. Zero at Nash equilibrium (2-player zero-sum).

**Extensive-form game.** A game described as a tree of decisions, chance events and payoffs.

**Information set.** A set of game states one player cannot distinguish.

**Mixed strategy.** Randomizing over pure strategies. Behavior and mixed strategies are equivalent under perfect recall.

**Nash equilibrium.** A strategy profile where nobody gains by deviating alone.

**Perfect recall.** Players never forget what they knew or did earlier. Needed for CFR.

**Range.** The distribution over hands a player might hold in a given situation.

**Regret.** How much better an action would have done than what was actually played.

**Regret matching.** Play actions in proportion to their positive cumulative regret.

**Zero-sum.** One player's gain is exactly the other's loss.
